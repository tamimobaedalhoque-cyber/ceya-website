We started Ceya because the best films we had worked on were heard before they were seen. So we built a house where sound sits at the table from the first meeting, not the last week.

Four layers, one house

  1. Concept

    Positioning, scripts and treatments. The idea, written down before anything is filmed.

    • Brand films
    • Campaigns
    • Series formats

  2. Story

    Characters, structure and pace. We write for the edit from the first day.

    • Documentary
    • Scripted
    • Podcasts

  3. Image

    Direction, cinematography, motion design and the Ceya grade.

    • Production
    • Motion
    • Colour

  4. Sound

    Score, sound design and mix. The layer people feel before they notice it.

    • Original score
    • Sound design
    • Final mix
